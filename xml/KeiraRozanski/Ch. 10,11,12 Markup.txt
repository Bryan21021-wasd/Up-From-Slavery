Ch. 10,11,12 Markup

1. First I searched for newlines and then replaced them to mark for paragraphs.
2. Next, I marked each chapter title as the chapter number and marked each chapter title as the chapter title.
3. Now I began the manual markup. When I came across a person's name, I searched for other occurrences of the name and replaced them with the correct tags. I marked names, places, themes, and feelings. 
	a.)Names and relations are tagged based on the information given. If it is a re-occurring person I tagged it with relations previously mentioned. If the name was mentioned once or just in a small section relation was tagged based on relevance to the story. For example, in chapters 11 and 12, multiple people are mentioned for making donations to the school. I marked their relations as "donors" since they do not seem to have any other form of relationship.
	b.) Places are marked based on the schema, with state or country attributes.
	c.) The project themes are marked based on the schema as either education or inequality. I left several comments clarifying my choices in marking passages as such. For the majority of the chapter education themes relate to Washington being a teacher. I marked passages that explicitly talk about what he is doing as an educator. Since the majority of these chapters talk about Washington seeking funding for the school I did not mark that as education. I marked inequality as examples of inequality for Washington or the school. I also marked and made comments toward passages that talk about the reverse effects of inequality. I think it would be important to compare situations of inequality and equality Washington faces throughout his life.
	d.)I marked feelings based on strong tones on specific passages. When a section had a strong underlying tone I marked it.
